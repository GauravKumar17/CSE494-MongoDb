A few things worth knowing.

What does the visit price include?

Each booking covers one hour of the provider’s listed service. Materials and extra work are excluded. Discuss and agree on them before work starts. The platform collects no commission or online payment.

Can I cancel a booking?

Yes. Customers can cancel requested or confirmed visits from the booking page. Once a visit has started, arrange any changes directly with the provider.

What does “profile reviewed” mean?

An administrator reviewed the submitted profile information. It does not certify qualifications, background or workmanship. Discuss your needs and safety requirements before agreeing to work.

How do I offer my services?

Sign up as a provider, complete your service profile and send it for review. Once approved, your profile appears in search.

Can I book work for a community space?

Yes. Tick the community-space option when requesting a visit and make sure you have permission to authorize work there.
